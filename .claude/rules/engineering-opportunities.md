# Engineering friction and automation opportunities

A standing practice, secondary to the current work. While working normally, notice material
friction that a script, an existing tool, an integration or a reusable developer tool might
remove: recurring manual work, confusing debugging, brittle integrations, and quality or
recovery problems. The evidence feeds a later choice of a third portfolio project, compared with
a separate log kept in Lost Minutes. That project need not involve dbt.

- Record substantive cases in `docs/ENGINEERING_OPPORTUNITIES.md`, following its rules and entry
  template. The log is not loaded automatically and must not be imported here. Read it when the
  work touches an area it covers, or before adding to it.
- Keep what was observed in this repository separate from any claim that others share the need.
  A broader need stays unvalidated until its demand test has run.
- Mark existing solutions NOT RESEARCHED until sources are cited with the date accessed. Never
  claim that nothing comparable exists without evidence.
- Add a repeat occurrence to an existing entry instead of creating a duplicate. Keep entries
  short.
- Current priorities come first. Keep fixing routine issues the current work needs. Do not start
  building the third project or add infrastructure only to generate ideas.
- Report correctness blockers immediately and track them in their ticket. The log may point at a
  blocker; it is never where a blocker waits.
- Do not assume access to Lost Minutes, and do not copy data between the two projects.
- At a milestone handoff, surface at most three material new opportunities, or say none emerged.
