# Ideas register

Candidate work, each with the question it would answer, what is known now, what it needs,
the smallest experiment that would settle it, and a criterion for stopping. **Recording an
idea here does not authorise implementing it.** Status moves only when a ticket exists.

Evidence labels follow the project's convention: MEASURED, HYPOTHESIS, UNKNOWN.

## Not ideas

Things that are excluded on purpose rather than pending: appliance detection, causal
claims about tariff response, presenting any scenario charge as a bill or a saving, and an
AI assistant that alters readings or calculates bills.
